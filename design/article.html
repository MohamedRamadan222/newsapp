NASA adjusts ISS flight schedule for upcoming missions

Sarah Johnson • Apr 15, 2026 • 5 min read

SJ NASA Newsroom Official source • 12k followers Follow

After reviewing the station flight schedule, NASA and its partners are adjusting launch opportunities for the next three crewed missions. The change gives teams extra time to complete vehicle checks and balance cargo deliveries.

The International Space Station continues to serve as a testbed for long-duration human spaceflight. Engineers say the new timeline reduces overlap between crew rotations and frees a docking port for an upcoming private astronaut mission.

Mission managers stressed that safety drove the decision. “We want every crew to launch with margin,” one official said. “This schedule keeps science running at full pace while we prepare for Artemis and beyond.”

The agency will publish the updated manifest this week, including new target dates for resupply flights and spacewalks focused on solar array upgrades.
